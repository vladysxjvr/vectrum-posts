What can I actually manage in Vectrum?

Your trucks, trailers, drivers, documents, service and daily transport operations, all in one place. Keep the information your team needs together instead of switching between spreadsheets and messengers.

How does vehicle service tracking work?

Vehicle records bring together service history, planned maintenance, documents and reported issues. Your team can see what needs attention and plan the next action.

Will Vectrum really save me time?

Connected orders, tracking, documents and communication reduce repeated data entry. Your team can spend more time organizing transport and less time looking for information.

What about communication with the team?

Company chat, files, photos and calls connect drivers and dispatchers in Vectrum Driver and Vectrum Logist.

Can I use Vectrum for my whole company?

Yes. Owners assign roles and individual access rights, so dispatchers, drivers, mechanics and finance teams work with the information they are authorized to see.

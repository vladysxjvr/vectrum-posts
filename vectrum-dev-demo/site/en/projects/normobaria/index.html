Krakowska Normobaria

In developmentThe project is still in development, so we show only selected screens.

Krakowska Normobaria is a mobile app for a normobaric therapy centre in Kraków. We designed and built it from scratch to replace paper passes: clients buy and keep passes in the app, book sessions and show a QR code at the reception.

Client
Normobaric therapy centre, Kraków
Platform
iOS app + admin panel
Years
2025–2026
Users
189 users

Key features

  • QR passes with remaining visits and validity dates
  • Session booking with a calendar and free places
  • Shop for passes and products, paid in złoty or “tlenki” points
  • “Tlenki” reward points for visits and purchases
  • News and push notifications
  • Admin panel for staff to mark visits

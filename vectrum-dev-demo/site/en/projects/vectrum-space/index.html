Vectrum Space

In developmentThe project is still in development, so we show only selected screens.

Vectrum Space is a transport management system for carriers. It brings orders, fleet, live tracking and drivers into one place, so dispatchers spend less time on paperwork and phone calls.

Type
B2B SaaS · TMS
Platform
Web app + mobile apps
Years
2026 – now
Status
Pilot on 3 trucks since October 2026

Key features

  • AI import of orders from documents: upload, recognition and review
  • Dispatch screen with AI risk hints and driver chat
  • Live tracking of active orders on a map
  • Fleet control: mileage, fuel, service history and documents
  • Overview dashboard: shipments, revenue, expenses and profit
  • Mobile apps for drivers and logisticians
